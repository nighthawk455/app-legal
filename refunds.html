Short version. Subscriptions and in-app purchases for these apps are sold by Apple through the App Store. We do not issue cash refunds ourselves. Purchases are generally final once access is delivered. Canceling stops future renewals; it does not automatically refund the current period. Apple may still grant a refund under Apple’s own rules. Nothing here limits rights you have under consumer law that cannot be waived.

1. Who bills you

Pro subscriptions for FrameMatch, LeaseLens, and PawRx are auto-renewable In-App Purchases billed by Apple. Apple (not Kaleb Eckhoff) is the merchant of record for those charges. There is no separate Stripe or web checkout in the current versions of these apps.

2. Our no-refund position (developer)

Except where required by law or by Apple’s policies, all sales are final. We do not provide cash refunds, credits, or prorated refunds for:

  • Monthly or yearly subscriptions after purchase or renewal
  • Unused time in a billing period after you cancel
  • Change of mind, unused features, or forgetting to cancel before renewal
  • Device changes, uninstalls, or loss of access caused by your Apple Account, device, or network

Because Apple processes payment, we also cannot reverse an App Store charge on our own. Asking us to “refund to my card” for an Apple In-App Purchase is something we are not able to do.

3. Canceling is not a refund

You may cancel auto-renew at any time in your Apple Account subscription settings (or via Manage Subscription in the app where provided). After you cancel:

  • You keep Pro access until the end of the paid (or trial) period you already started
  • You will not be charged for the next renewal
  • Canceling does not by itself refund amounts already charged

Apple’s help for canceling: support.apple.com/en-us/118428

4. Free trials

Where a yearly plan includes a free trial configured in App Store Connect, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends (or as Apple’s rules require) if you do not want to be charged. Starting or finishing a free trial does not create a separate cash refund right from us if the paid period then begins under Apple’s terms.

5. Requesting a refund from Apple

If you believe a charge should be refunded, request it from Apple:

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com
  2. Sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase
  3. Choose “Request a refund,” pick a reason, select the item, and submit

Apple decides refund eligibility under the Apple Media Services Terms and applicable law. Eligibility can vary by country or region. See also Apple’s refund help.

If Apple grants a refund, Pro access for the refunded purchase may be removed in line with Apple’s and our entitlement systems.

6. How we may respond when Apple asks about a refund

When someone requests a refund, Apple may ask us for purchase or usage context (consumption information). By using the apps and making purchases, you consent to us providing Apple with information reasonably needed to respond to that request, consistent with our Privacy Policy and Apple’s requirements. Apple still makes the final refund decision.

7. Defects and support

If the app fails to deliver purchased Pro access after a successful Apple charge, contact us at kmeckhoff81@gmail.com with enough detail to investigate (approximate purchase time, product, and device). We will help with Restore Purchases and entitlement issues. That support is not a promise of a cash refund from us; billing disputes for App Store charges still go through Apple.

8. Statutory rights

If you are a consumer in a place with mandatory protections (for example certain rights in the EU/EEA/UK, Australia, New Zealand, or elsewhere), nothing in this Refund Policy limits those rights to the extent they cannot be waived. Where those rights apply to an App Store purchase, exercise them through Apple’s process and/or contact us so we can point you to the right channel.

9. Missouri / US note

These apps are developed in Missouri, USA. A no-refund stance for digital subscriptions is common when access has been delivered, but unfair or deceptive practices remain unlawful under laws such as the Missouri Merchandising Practices Act. We aim to disclose subscription price, auto-renewal, trial length, and cancellation clearly before purchase.
