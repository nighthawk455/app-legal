Privacy Policy — FrameMatch

Operator: Kaleb Eckhoff (individual developer/publisher)

Contact: legal@kalebapps.com

1. Who we are

FrameMatch is a consumer iOS app published by Kaleb Eckhoff. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use FrameMatch.

2. What the app does

FrameMatch helps you capture guided progress photos (for example fitness, skincare, or renovation projects), organize them into projects, compare shots, and optionally receive local weekly shoot reminders. There is no account or login, and FrameMatch does not operate its own cloud backend in this version.

3. Information stored on your device

The following categories of information are stored on your device only (for example as local JSON metadata and photo files):

  • Photos you capture with the camera or import from your photo library
  • Project and shot metadata (names, dates, alignment/compare settings, reminder preferences)
  • App preferences such as onboarding completion and reminder schedule

We do not receive or host your progress photos or project data on our servers because FrameMatch has no developer-operated backend in this version.

4. Information processed by Apple and RevenueCat (purchases)

If you purchase or restore a subscription, payment is handled by Apple through In-App Purchase. FrameMatch uses RevenueCat to manage entitlements. In connection with purchases, Apple and/or RevenueCat may process:

  • Purchase and subscription status
  • Anonymous app user identifiers used for entitlement checks
  • Device and transaction information needed to fulfill and restore purchases

Prices shown in the app come from StoreKit localized price strings. There is no Stripe or web billing in this version. Apple’s and RevenueCat’s own privacy policies also apply to their processing.

5. Categories summary

  • Photos: stored on device
  • Documents / lease text: not used by FrameMatch
  • Pet / medication information: not used by FrameMatch
  • Device / purchase identifiers: may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat for subscriptions
  • Diagnostics / analytics SDKs: none operated by us in this version

6. No sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because FrameMatch keeps user content on device and has no developer backend, we do not monetize your photos or project data.

7. Tracking and advertising

In this version, FrameMatch does not engage in App Tracking Transparency (ATT) tracking, does not use advertising identifiers for ads, and does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

8. Children’s privacy (COPPA)

FrameMatch is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help you understand available local controls (such as deleting app data or uninstalling).

9. Retention

  • On-device data: remains on your device until you delete projects/photos in the app, clear app data, or uninstall FrameMatch.
  • Purchase records: retained by Apple (and related purchase infrastructure such as RevenueCat) according to their practices and applicable law.

10. Your controls

  • Delete projects and photos within the app
  • Disable local reminders in Settings (where available)
  • Uninstall the app to remove remaining on-device app data
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings
  • Use Restore Purchases if you reinstall or change devices

11. Account deletion

Not applicable. FrameMatch does not create user accounts in this version.

12. Third parties

Relevant third parties for this version are Apple (App Store, StoreKit billing, system services such as camera/photo library/notifications) and RevenueCat (subscription entitlement management). We do not operate a separate analytics, crash, or cloud-sync provider for FrameMatch in this version.

13. International users

FrameMatch is offered primarily for users in the United States. If you use the app from elsewhere, your on-device data remains on your device; purchase processing follows Apple’s and RevenueCat’s regional practices.

14. California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to opt out of sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are commonly understood for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because most FrameMatch content never leaves your device, many requests are best fulfilled by using in-app delete controls or uninstalling. For questions about purchase-related data held by Apple or RevenueCat, contact them or us at the email below and we will point you to the appropriate channel.

15. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of FrameMatch after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
