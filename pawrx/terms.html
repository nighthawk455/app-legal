Data is stored on your device and stays there unless you choose to export an encrypted household package (caregiver names and an invite code) or a passphrase-protected backup file (which can contain pet and medication data) and share it yourself through the iOS share sheet (for example AirDrop, Messages, or Files). We never receive these files; whoever you share them with and the service you use to send them handle them under their own terms. You are responsible for keeping your passphrase and invite code private. There is no account/login and no developer-operated cloud backend in this version.

Important — not veterinary advice. FurDose is for reminders and personal tracking only. It is not veterinary advice, not a dosage calculator, and not a tool for diagnosis or treatment. Always follow instructions from a licensed veterinarian. Do not change a pet’s medication based solely on this app.
