1. Who we are

LeaseLens is a consumer iOS app published by Kaleb Eckhoff. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use LeaseLens.

2. What the app does

LeaseLens helps renters import a lease (PDF or photos) and run an on-device heuristic analysis that highlights common patterns for educational review. Analyses are stored locally. There is no account or login, and LeaseLens does not operate its own cloud backend in this version. An optional “AI enhancer” may appear in the product UI but is a no-op stub in this version and does not send lease text to cloud AI services.

3. Information stored on your device

The following categories of information are stored on your device only:

  • Lease documents and images you import (PDF/photos)
  • Extracted or entered lease text used for on-device heuristic analysis
  • Analysis results and related metadata saved locally
  • App preferences such as onboarding and free-tier usage flags

We do not upload your lease documents or analysis results to developer-operated servers in this version.

4. Information processed by Apple and RevenueCat (purchases)

If you purchase or restore a subscription, payment is handled by Apple through In-App Purchase. LeaseLens uses RevenueCat to manage entitlements. In connection with purchases, Apple and/or RevenueCat may process purchase/subscription status, anonymous app user identifiers, and device/transaction information needed to fulfill and restore purchases. Prices shown in the app come from StoreKit localized price strings. There is no Stripe or web billing in this version.

5. Categories summary

  • Photos / documents / lease text: stored and analyzed on device
  • Pet / medication information: not used by LeaseLens
  • Device / purchase identifiers: may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat for subscriptions
  • Cloud AI processing of lease content: not performed in this version
  • Diagnostics / analytics SDKs: none operated by us in this version

6. No sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Lease content stays on your device in this version.

7. Tracking and advertising

In this version, LeaseLens does not engage in App Tracking Transparency (ATT) tracking, does not use advertising identifiers for ads, and does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

8. Children’s privacy (COPPA)

LeaseLens is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Retention

  • On-device data: remains until you clear data in Settings (where available), delete analyses, or uninstall LeaseLens.
  • Purchase records: retained by Apple (and related purchase infrastructure such as RevenueCat) according to their practices.

10. Your controls

  • Clear local data in Settings (where the app provides that control)
  • Uninstall the app to remove remaining on-device app data
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings
  • Use Restore Purchases if you reinstall or change devices

11. Account deletion

Not applicable. LeaseLens does not create user accounts in this version.

12. Third parties

Relevant third parties for this version are Apple and RevenueCat. We do not operate a separate analytics, crash, cloud-sync, or cloud-AI provider for LeaseLens lease content in this version.

13. International users

LeaseLens is offered primarily for users in the United States. Lease and landlord-tenant laws vary significantly by jurisdiction. On-device data remains on your device; purchase processing follows Apple’s and RevenueCat’s practices.

14. California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to opt out of sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because lease content generally never leaves your device, many requests are best fulfilled by clearing local data or uninstalling. For purchase-related data held by Apple or RevenueCat, contact them or us and we will point you to the appropriate channel.

15. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
